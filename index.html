    Koffeeyap

    Featured Project

    Founder & Product Lead

    2024 – Present

    Problem

    Calgary has a vibrant coffee culture and professional community, but no dedicated way for people to discover and connect over coffee meetups in real life. Existing platforms were too generic or focused on dating rather than genuine networking and community building.

    Process

    Designed and built the full product from the ground up — conducting user research with local coffee enthusiasts, mapping user journeys, creating wireframes and prototypes in Figma, and developing the web platform. Established strategic partnerships with Calgary cafés to create authentic meetup venues and cultivated a network of ambassadors and creators to drive community growth.

    Outcome

    Launched koffeeyap.ca as a live platform connecting Calgarians through coffee meetups. Successfully partnered with local cafés and built an initial community of users and ambassadors, validating product-market fit through real-world usage and positive community feedback.

    Circle App

    Team Lead & Product Designer

    natIgnite Hackathon — Top 3 · Aug 2025

    Problem

    Seniors often face social isolation and struggle to use complex technology. Existing social apps were not designed with accessibility or the specific needs of older adults in mind.

    Process

    Led a 3-person team through rapid ideation, user research, and prototype development. Prioritized accessibility throughout the design process — large touch targets, high-contrast UI, simple navigation, and clear visual hierarchy. Conducted user testing with seniors to validate design decisions.

    Outcome

    Placed in the Top 3 at natIgnite. Post-event user surveys showed 90% positive feedback from senior testers, validating our accessibility-first approach and user-centered design process.
